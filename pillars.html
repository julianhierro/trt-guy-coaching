What we're actually building

The Four Pillars

Every plan I write is aimed at these four things. Not a look for one weekend in summer — a body that works, and keeps working, for the next thirty years.

01

Improved body composition

I'll get you to a level of body fat that's actually healthy.

Not shredded for one photo and miserable by Thursday. A body fat level you can hold all year without living out of a Tupperware or turning down dinner with your family.

That's usually the difference between a man who looks like he trains and a man who's just thinner than he used to be.

How we track it: waist, weight and photos — not the scale alone
02

Gain muscle mass

The goal isn't to look like a bodybuilder. It's strong, lean and healthy.

For that you need a real amount of muscle — enough that your shirt sits the way you want it to, and enough that everyday life never becomes a problem.

Carrying both kids up the stairs, moving furniture on a Saturday, getting off the floor without thinking about it. Muscle is what makes all of that unremarkable.

How we track it: strength going up while your waist doesn't
03

Cardiovascular capacity

Heart disease is the leading cause of death in the world. A strong engine is the best protection you can build.

This is the pillar men skip because it isn't visible in the mirror. It's also the one most likely to decide how long you're around for.

You'll feel it long before any test does — stairs stop registering, you're not wrecked after a game with your kids, and your resting heart rate quietly drops.

How we track it: resting heart rate, and what you can do without stopping
04

Bloodwork and hormones

Everything above is guesswork until someone reads your labs properly.

Your panel tells us what's actually happening — hormones, thyroid, liver, lipids, markers most men have never had explained to them. Your supplements get chosen from that, not from a shelf.

I don't prescribe and I won't pretend to. What I do is read it, tell you what I'd be asking about, and give you something specific to take back to your doctor.

How we track it: a panel before we start, and again 6–8 weeks in

They only work together

Lose fat without building muscle and you end up smaller, softer and weaker. Build muscle without the engine and you get out of breath carrying it. Do all of it without reading your bloodwork and you're guessing at the one thing you can't see.
