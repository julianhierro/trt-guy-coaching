Before you ask

FAQs

Do I have to be on TRT to work with you?

No. A lot of the men I work with are not on it, some are still deciding, and some never end up using it. The program works whether you're on TRT or not. 

Do you prescribe testosterone, or tell me what to take?

No. I'm a coach, not a doctor, and I won't pretend otherwise. What I will do is read your labs properly, tell you what I'd be asking about, and give you something specific to bring back to whoever prescribes for you.

What do I actually get for $125 a week?

Your training program built around the equipment and the schedule you actually have. Nutrition that fits how you eat. Your bloodwork read and explained. Chat support six days a week. A weekly check-in where we look at what happened and adjust. Peptide protocols if you want them and they make sense for you.

How does the billing work?

Weekly. No contract, no minimum term — cancel whenever you want and the billing stops.

I travel constantly / work nights. Wil. 

No, that's most of my clients. The plan gets built around the week you actually have, hotel gyms and airport food included. If a week falls apart, we adjust it instead of you starting over on Monday.

Do I need a gym?

No. Tell me what you've got — a full gym, a garage, a pair of dumbbells — and the program gets written for that.

How long before I see anything?

Strength and energy usually move first, inside the first month. Anything you'd want to photograph takes longer than that. If someone promises you a number by a date, they're guessing.

What if it turns out not to be for me?

Cancel inside the first seven days and you get your money back. After that you can still cancel any week you like — you just keep what you've paid for.
